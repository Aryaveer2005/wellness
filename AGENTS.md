All project rules live in **`CLAUDE.md`** — read it first and follow it exactly.

**Mandatory after every change you make:** update **`PROJECT_STATUS.md`**
(the "Last updated" line, the feature table, known issues, and one new line at the top of the Activity Log).
Approved scope or doc changes also get a line in `docs/CHANGELOG.md`.
